Explore three illustrative ways a $15 million water revenue bond could affect monthly water bills. Select a scenario below to change bond assumptions and view the estimated customer impact.

All Water Meters

Spread the bond payment equally across the City's projected 103,659 water meters. A straightforward benchmark for the potential monthly impact.

Commercial & Industrial Only

Exclude an estimated 85,026 residential meters and spread the bond payment across an estimated 18,633 commercial and industrial meters. Defaults to a 20-year term.

SCENARIO 03

Weighted by Meter Size

Exempt meters smaller than 2 inches. Allocate the bond payment among 2-inch and larger meters using adjustable size weights and assumed meter-size distribution. Defaults to a 20-year term.

Open weighted calculator
How to interpret the results: These are hypothetical cost-allocation scenarios, not adopted City rates. Meter-size shares and residential/commercial counts are estimates. The models assume that the entire annual bond payment is recovered through additional water-meter charges; actual City financing and rate decisions could differ.
